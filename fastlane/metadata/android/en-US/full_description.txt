Warden is a privacy-first firewall for Android that needs no root.

It uses Android's local VPN slot to see every connection your apps make, explains it in plain words, and lets you block trackers per app. Nothing leaves your phone: there is no account, no analytics and no cloud.

Features:
* Live log of every DNS lookup and connection, labelled by app
* Per-app detail: domains reached, expandable connections, activity
* Block or allow any domain for one app or everywhere
* Per-app Wi-Fi and mobile data blocking
* Downloadable blocklists (HaGeZi, StevenBlack, OISD)
* Live globe showing which countries your apps talk to, filterable by app
* Warns when strict Private DNS bypasses filtering
* Light and dark themes

Warden never decrypts HTTPS: it sees where apps connect, not what they send.
